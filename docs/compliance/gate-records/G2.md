## What "listed, stocked, and sold" means here

Unlike G0/G1 (tooling/schema gates), G2 is the first feature-level gate: it requires a real buyer-facing capability to work end-to-end through the actual service layer, not just for each underlying piece to exist in isolation. The individual pieces were each built and tested by their own card (`ListingService`/`ProductListingService` — D023/D032, `VariantService` — D033, `InventoryService`/stock invariant I5 — D039/D040, `ProductSaleService` — D042), but no prior card had exercised all four together in one flow.

## Verification performed this session

`packages/db/src/services/gate-g2.e2e.test.ts` (3 cases, new this card) wires the real service classes together — `ProductListingService` → `VariantService` → `InventoryService` → `ProductSaleService` — with no direct repository/schema writes standing in for any step of the flow itself (repository classes are only used to construct the services and to seed the merchant/user/location fixture rows a real onboarding flow would already have created):

1. **"lists, stocks, and sells a product entirely through the service layer"** — creates a `PRODUCT` listing and a priced variant, receives 10 units of stock, sells 2 then 8 (draining stock to exactly 0), asserts the ledger-derived stock (`InventoryService.getStock`) is correct at every step, asserts a third sale against zero stock is rejected with `InsufficientStockError` (invariant I5, still enforced), and asserts the full write trail (one receipt, two sales) is captured in the hash-chained audit log and that `verifyAuditChain` reports it clean.
2. **"rejects a sale where a caller-supplied price would have undercut the server's"** — a raw (non-typed) sale payload carries `priceMinor: 1`/`unitPriceMinor: 1`; the recorded sale still prices at the variant's real `10000`, proving the server-authoritative pricing rule (`CLAUDE.md`: "prices and totals are always recomputed server-side") holds across the full flow, not just inside `ProductSaleService`'s own unit tests.
3. **"a READ_ONLY role cannot stock or sell a product unaided"** — both `InventoryService.receiveStock` and `ProductSaleService.sellProduct` reject a `READ_ONLY` acting role, proving RBAC (D037) is actually enforced along this path, not bypassable by calling the services directly.

## Known gaps, honestly stated

- This flow sells directly against a variant/location (a register-style sale), the same shape `ProductSaleService` (D042) already used — it does not yet go through `CartService` (D043)'s cart→checkout path. No card in the sequence yet defines a cart-to-order "checkout" transition (`BUILD_STATE.md`'s D043 carried-over note flags this explicitly); G2's criterion is satisfied by the direct-sale path, which is a real, already-shipped way to sell a product, not a stand-in.
- No HTTP route exists yet for any of `ProductListingService`/`VariantService`/`InventoryService`/`ProductSaleService` — same "service exists, no route wired" gap already tracked since D040–D043. G2's criterion is about the product/inventory domain capability existing and being provably correct, which this test demonstrates; wiring it to the web is Phase 2's `apps/api` follow-on, tracked separately, not part of this gate's wording.
- As with every gate to date, "PASSED" here means the full local command sequence, not a fresh GitHub Actions run — the standing gap already documented in G0's own record.

## Verdict

**PASSED.** A product can be listed (`ProductListingService`), stocked (`InventoryService.receiveStock`, against the real append-only ledger and I5's non-negative invariant), and sold (`ProductSaleService.sellProduct`, server-priced, RBAC-gated) — proven by a real, passing, service-layer-only end-to-end test, not by inspecting each piece separately. Phase 2 (`D031`–`D044`) is closed; Phase 3 (`D045`–`D062`, availability engine and services, Gate G3) is next.
