## What happened

Built push notifications for confirmations, reminders, and return-due — the fourth channel alongside email (D134). New pure `packages/domain/src/notifications/pushNotifications.ts` mirrors `transactionalNotifications.ts`'s four content builders but deliberately strips money (`amountMinor`/`currency`/formatted totals) and raw internal ids from the visible `title`/`body`, keeping only opaque routing ids in a `data` field — a push notification is rendered by a third-party transport (APNs/FCM) and commonly shown on a locked device's screen, so it gets a stricter content policy than an email body. New `packages/db` layer: `pushDeviceTokens` schema (sqlite + pg, migrations `0022_wet_piledriver.sql`/`0024_lucky_logan.sql`) storing one row per device, unique per `(merchant_id, token)` so a re-registering device upserts rather than duplicating; `PushDeviceTokenRepository` (tenant-scoped, `register`/`listForCustomer`); `PushNotificationDispatcher`, which iterates every device a customer has registered and fires an injectable `send` callback once per device — same "delivery out of scope, no live provider" posture as every other notifier in this codebase (D069/D076/D094/D118/D130/D134), but the dispatch loop itself is real and doesn't branch on `platform` at all, which is exactly what proves this card's acceptance criterion. New `POST /v1/m/{slug}/account/push-tokens` (`apps/api/src/routes/pushTokens.ts`), same session-Bearer/cookie posture as `customerOrders.ts` — a device token can only ever be registered against the identity that owns the presenting session, never a client-supplied customer id. Wired the push channel into the one trigger point that already existed end-to-end: `OrderConfirmationService.confirmOrder` now also fires an `OrderConfirmationPushNotifier` unconditionally (unlike the email notifier, which is gated on `contactEmail` — a customer with no registered device simply has nothing to dispatch to, handled inside the dispatcher, not the call site); `orderConfirmation.ts`'s route composes the real dispatcher from `PushDeviceTokenRepository` + `PushNotificationDispatcher` + `buildOrderConfirmationPush`. New mobile `apps/mobile/src/notifications/registerPushToken.ts` (DI'd `fetchFn`/`accessToken`, same convention as every mobile fetch helper since D140) posts a device's platform/token through one call shape with no platform branching — proven identical for `ios` and `android` in its own tests. Reminder (`RESERVATION_REMINDER`) and return-due (`RETURN_DUE`) push content builders exist and are tested, but — same as D134's own email equivalents — have no scheduler trigger point to fire from yet (no scheduler infrastructure exists anywhere in this codebase); wiring them is a small follow-on once D134's `ReservationReminderJob`/`ReturnDueJob` get invoked by something.

## Security check answer

"No sensitive detail in notification payloads." By construction: every push builder in `pushNotifications.ts` omits `amountMinor`/`currency`/formatted totals and any raw internal id from the visible `title`/`body` — only an opaque `data.orderId`/`data.reservationId` for the app's own deep-link, never rendered as text. Proven directly in `pushNotifications.test.ts` (each builder's output asserted not to contain the amount, currency, or raw reservation id) and end-to-end in `apps/api/test/orderConfirmation.test.ts`'s new case, which confirms a real order with a 10000-minor-unit USD total produces push content containing neither `"10000"`, `"100.00"`, nor `"USD"`.
